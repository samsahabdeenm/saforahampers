WE RESPECT YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy policy

This page explains how information is handled when you browse the Safora Hampers website or prepare a gifting enquiry.

Information you provide

If you complete the enquiry form, you may enter your name, email address, occasion, approximate quantity and message. The form prepares an enquiry message for you to copy and send through Safora’s Instagram profile. The website does not submit that information to a Safora server.

Where your information goes

If you choose to send the prepared message through Instagram, the information you choose to share is handled by Instagram under its own privacy terms. Please review the platform’s policies before sending personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently include a configured analytics platform or a custom advertising tracker. Embedded or linked third-party services may apply their own policies when you visit them.

Data minimisation

Please share only the details needed to discuss your gifting request. Do not include payment card details, passwords or other sensitive information in the enquiry.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the website’s enquiry process or services change. This general notice is not a substitute for legal advice.
