A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Good to know.

Have another question? Share it with us in your enquiry.

Can I request a fully customised hamper?+

Yes. Share your occasion, preferences, theme and any ideas you have. We can discuss a custom concept around your requirements.

Do you take corporate and bulk orders?+

Yes, you can enquire about employee gifting, client gifts, events, branded concepts and bulk quantities. Share your approximate quantity and timeline so the requirements can be discussed.

How do I get pricing?+

Pricing depends on the selection, personalisation, quantity and requirements. Send an enquiry for a quote tailored to your request.

Can gifts be planned for a particular event or date?+

Share your event date and expected quantity as early as possible. The available timeline and order details can then be confirmed with you.

Where is Safora Hampers based?+

Safora Hampers is based in Chennai. Contact us to discuss your gifting requirements and delivery or fulfilment details.

How do I start an order?+

Complete the enquiry form with your contact details, occasion and ideas. It will prepare a message you can copy and send to Safora through Instagram.
